# Alert sound credits

Focus / break completion sounds. All are royalty-free, sourced from Wikimedia
Commons and re-processed (downmixed to mono, resampled to 22050 Hz, trimmed,
high-passed, noise-gated where the field recording was hissy, normalized and
faded) into the short 16-bit WAV clips shipped here.
