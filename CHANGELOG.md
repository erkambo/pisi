# Changelog

What changed in each release. Downloads and install notes are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/erkambo/pisi/releases).

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-06)

The first public release. New since the private builds:

- **Meet PISI's new cat:** a chunky tuxedo with white socks. Pet Studio makes
  any other look (shuffle, or build your own), and if you chose the black cat
  before, you keep it.
- **Focus guard.** Mark a site as distracting in the extension's menu (one
  click for YouTube, Reddit, X and the like, or right-click any page); during
  a focus block the cat sits in the middle of it and the page softly fades.
  Drag it off for five minutes, then three, then one. The extension opens a
  welcome page when you install it, and PISI says so when a guarded site
  can't be guarded.
- **It knocks your words off the page.** The cat walks to the end of a line,
  looks at you, and swats the last word over the edge (now and then it digs
  through a paragraph instead); *Tidy up this page* puts them back.
- **Page reactions.** It pounces on selections, watches videos, bobs to music,
  naps on slow reads, stretches at the end of a page, startles on zoom, leaps
  off a closing tab, covers its eyes on login pages and stalks your pointer.
- **New poses** drawn by the engine: dig, cover eyes, startle, bob, sniff.
- **Right-click on a page** to throw a toy or shine the laser there.
- **Tables are steps:** the cat climbs them row by row.
- Fixes: scrolling no longer carries the cat off the top of the window; pages
  are found even when Brave reports a made-up screen size.
- Security: a private data folder, private local sockets, plain-text calendar
  titles, a doctor report without personal details, hardened CI, and releases
  with checksums and build provenance.

## Before 1.0

PISI was shared privately with friends as 1.1 (Windows), 1.2 (macOS) and 1.3.

### 1.3.1 (2026-10-05)

- Google Calendar in one click, with the narrowest permission: PISI only uses
  a calendar of its own.
- The extension's icon shows whether the cat can use the page you're on.
- A new tutorial, Pet Studio from Settings, and smooth Pet Studio sliders.
- Clicking the toy basket starts play; crumbs and splashes at the bowl.
- PISI draws every cat itself and is MIT licensed.
- Fixes: pausing a focus block wakes the cat; the energy bar refills after play.

### 1.3.0 and earlier

PISI's corner, the shop (40 things), panel extensions for Cinnamon and GNOME,
and the Windows and macOS installers.
