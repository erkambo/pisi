# Code of conduct

PISI is a small, friendly project, and we'd like it to feel that way for
everyone who takes part, whoever they are and whatever their experience.

## Please do

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take it the same way.
- Keep discussions on topic and easy to follow.

## Please don't

- Harass, insult, threaten or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Make sexual, discriminatory or otherwise hurtful comments or images.
- Share someone's private information without their permission.
- Keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the
project.

## If something goes wrong

Email the maintainer at
[boyaciogluerkam@gmail.com](mailto:boyaciogluerkam@gmail.com). Reports are
read promptly and kept confidential. Depending on what happened, the
response can be a word in private, a public note, removing content, or
removing someone from the project, temporarily or for good.
